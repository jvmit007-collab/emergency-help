Basic First-Aid Guide

For serious emergencies, contact trained emergency services immediately.

🩸 Severe Bleeding

Apply firm pressure with clean cloth or gauze. Keep pressure until help arrives.

🔥 Burns

Cool a minor burn under clean running water. Do not apply ice, butter, or toothpaste.

😵 Unconsciousness

Check responsiveness and breathing. Call emergency services and follow the operator's instructions.

🦴 Fracture

Keep the injured area still. Do not try to straighten the bone. Seek medical help.
